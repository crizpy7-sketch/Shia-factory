# GARY-001 Multi-Model Research Intake

Use this file when you bring research back from multiple models. Do **not** ask the models to simply imitate Gary Vaynerchuk. Ask them to research documented public principles and provide sources.

For each research model, collect:

## 1. Model / research packet
- Model name and version:
- Date researched:
- Search/web access used: yes/no
- Primary sources consulted:
- Secondary sources consulted:

## 2. Claims worth considering
For every meaningful claim include:
- Claim:
- Source URL/title/date:
- Primary or secondary source:
- Direct evidence or model inference:
- Confidence: high / medium / low
- Does another research model agree?:
- Contradictions or caveats:

## 3. Categories to research
- Marketing philosophy
- Brand building
- Attention and distribution
- Social/content strategy
- Platform-native creative
- Customer/community philosophy
- Organic versus paid acquisition
- Experimentation and measurement
- Entrepreneurship and speed
- Leadership and communication
- Long-term versus short-term tradeoffs
- Documented mistakes or changed beliefs
- Principles that aged well
- Tactics that were context-specific and should not become permanent rules

## 4. Personality evidence
Separate documented communication patterns from caricature. Capture:
- directness
- energy
- optimism
- competitiveness
- empathy/customer orientation
- tolerance for disagreement
- approach to criticism
- recurring rhetorical habits

Do not turn profanity, celebrity mannerisms, or catchphrases into mandatory behavior.

## 5. Final synthesis request
Ask each model to end with:
- 10 highest-confidence principles
- 5 disputed or uncertain claims
- 5 things an AI agent should **not** imitate
- recommended sources to verify next

When you return these packets, they can be merged into `identity/cognitive_model.md`, logged in `knowledge/research_ledger.jsonl`, and used to build Gary's certification exam.
