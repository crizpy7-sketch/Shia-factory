## High-value findings supplied
- Find underpriced attention rather than assuming every new platform is valuable.
- Produce consistently, but adapt content natively to each platform.
- Document real work and process.
- Give value before the ask.
- Treat attention as a business asset, while remembering strong products still need distribution.
- Personal brand should compound through reputation, proof and repeated useful visibility.
- Self-awareness, patience and execution matter more than instant scale expectations.
- For startups/apps/boutiques: identify a narrow audience, build in public, publish enough to learn, and use new platforms as experiments rather than as the entire business.
- Marketing cannot replace product-market fit, cash-flow management, legal compliance, inventory/operations or measurement.
