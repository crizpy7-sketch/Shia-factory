# Verification Report — GARY-GEMINI-001

**Review date:** 2026-08-13  
**Decision:** PARTIALLY ACCEPTED / PRIMARY-SOURCE VERIFIED PRINCIPLES ADDED

## Accepted with strong support

1. **Underpriced attention is a durable GaryVee principle.**
   - First-party GaryVee biography explicitly describes growing the family business through “underpriced attention,” including ecommerce, email marketing, content, and Google AdWords.
   - A dedicated GaryVee article defines underpriced attention as attention in channels not yet fully understood or efficiently priced by decision makers.

2. **Wine Library transformation and Wine Library TV are verified at a high level.**
   - First-party biography says he transformed Shoppers Discount Liquors into Wine Library, an early alcohol ecommerce business, and launched Wine Library TV on YouTube in 2006.
   - Exact starting revenue is conflicted across first-party GaryVee pages ($3M vs $4M). Preserve only “grew to about $60M annual sales” unless the discrepancy is resolved.

3. **VaynerMedia was co-founded in 2009 with AJ Vaynerchuk.**
   - Supported by GaryVee first-party biography and VaynerMedia-associated material.

4. **Interest-media / creative-relevance shift is current and directly supported.**
   - NRF 2026 reporting quotes Vaynerchuk explaining that AI-driven social algorithms prioritize relevance and that reach increasingly belongs to the individual creative rather than follower count.

5. **Document, Don't Create is directly supported.**
   - GaryVee's own 2016 article explicitly teaches documenting the process as a practical way to create frequent authentic content.

6. **Pillar-content repurposing / reverse-pyramid content strategy is directly supported.**
   - GaryVee's 2019 content-strategy article describes starting with pillar content and repurposing it into dozens of smaller, platform-contextual pieces.

7. **Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook is directly supported.**
   - GaryVee's own explanation defines jabs as customer value and the right hook as the ask, while stressing that value does not guarantee conversion and that marketers must earn the right to ask.
